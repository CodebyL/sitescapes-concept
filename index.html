One studio, from sketch to stone.

Sitescapes is listed for both landscape design and landscape construction. Keeping them together means the people who decided why a path bends are the ones who make sure it does.

Landscape design

A plan starts with how the property already works: where the light falls, how water moves, which trees are worth keeping and how the household uses the yard. The design then sets out rooms, routes and planting so the garden reads as one composition through the year.

  • Site study
  • Master plan
  • Planting design

Landscape construction

Construction carries the drawing into the ground: grading, hardscape, planting and the finishing details that decide whether a garden looks considered or simply completed. Fewer handoffs, fewer surprises.

  • Terraces and walks
  • Walls and steps
  • Planting to plan

Service details beyond design and construction are illustrative in this concept, to confirm with the studio.

How a project moves.

A typical path from first call to a garden that has settled in. Timing depends on the property and the season.

  1. Site visit

    Walk the property and note light, grade, views and the trees worth keeping.

  2. Plan

    Lay out rooms and routes: a terrace off the house, a walk that takes the long way round.

  3. Plant palette

    Trees, shrubs and perennials chosen so each season has something to look at.

  4. Build

    Grading, stone and drainage go in first, then the planting.

  5. First season

    Watering, edging and first blooms while the garden settles in.
